# Product Brief

## Overview

Email address: cloud@oreva.ai

**Product Name:** Oreva  
**Category:** AI-Powered Project Planning Assistant  
**Target Users:** Product managers, project managers, and process managers  
**Status:** Coming Soon - Currently in Development

Oreva is an AI assistant that will help project managers create comprehensive project plans through intelligent brainstorming sessions. Once launched, it will connect to your existing tools (Jira, Linear, Notion, etc.) and collaborate with you to design detailed plans with milestones, tasks, assignees, due dates, and estimates. After implementation, it will monitor progress and suggest updates to keep your projects on track.

**Join our waitlist to be notified when Oreva launches and get early access.**

---

## Core Functionality

### 1. Intelligent Brainstorming
- Connect your task management tools (Jira, Linear, etc.) and documentation (Notion, Confluence, etc.)
- Engage in conversational planning sessions with AI that understands your team and context
- Access your existing knowledge base to inform planning decisions
- Iterate on ideas until the plan is exactly what you need

### 2. Visual Plan Creation
- AI presents plans on an interactive whiteboard/canvas
- Manual editing capabilities for fine-tuning
- Conversational editing - ask the AI to modify specific aspects
- Clear visualization of milestones, tasks, dependencies, and timelines

### 3. Plan Implementation
- Direct integration with your chosen project management tool
- Automatic creation of tasks with proper assignees, due dates, and estimates
- Human approval required before any changes are made
- Seamless transition from planning to execution

### 4. Ongoing Progress Monitoring
- Daily progress tracking and status updates
- Proactive identification of potential issues or delays
- Suggested task status updates and plan adjustments
- All suggestions require human approval before implementation

---

## User Workflow

1. **Connect Tools**: Link Jira/Linear and document repositories
2. **Brainstorm**: Discuss project goals and requirements with AI
3. **Plan Creation**: AI generates visual project plan with milestones and tasks
4. **Iterate**: Refine plan through conversation or manual edits
5. **Implement**: AI creates actual tasks in your project management tool
6. **Monitor**: AI tracks progress and suggests updates (with approval)

## Key Features

### Planning Phase
- **Context-Aware AI**: Understands your team, past projects, and available resources
- **Document Integration**: References existing documentation and knowledge base
- **Visual Planning**: Interactive whiteboard for plan visualization and editing
- **Iterative Refinement**: Multiple rounds of improvement until plan is perfect
- **Complete Task Details**: Assignees, estimates, due dates, dependencies

### Implementation Phase
- **Tool Integration**: Direct connection to Jira, Linear, and other PM tools
- **Automated Task Creation**: Bulk creation of properly configured tasks
- **Human Control**: All implementations require explicit approval
- **Progress Tracking**: Continuous monitoring of task completion and timeline adherence

### Monitoring Phase
- **Daily Check-ins**: Regular progress assessment and status updates
- **Proactive Alerts**: Early warning of potential delays or issues
- **Adaptive Planning**: Suggestions for plan adjustments based on actual progress
- **Approval-Based Updates**: All changes require human confirmation
